Personalized Therapeutics

Working with Specialized Compounding Pharmacies

Every patient's biology is unique. Standard, commercial mass-produced pharmaceuticals often come in fixed dosages and with fillers that may cause side effects or allergies.

At Cadence Clinic, we collaborate with premier compounding pharmacies to design and formulate custom prescriptions. This customized medical pharmacy approach allows us to:

Custom Strengths & Dosages

Adjusting active ingredient levels specifically to your blood lab numbers, minimizing side effects and optimizing efficacy.

Combined Formulations

Blending multiple compatible active medications into a single pill, cream, or sublingual troche for convenience.

Hypoallergenic Formulations

Removing undesirable excipients, dyes, gluten, lactose, or preservatives to accommodate sensitive individuals.

Specialized Delivery Methods

Transforming medications from hard-to-swallow pills into topical transdermal gels, oral liquids, or sublingual options.
